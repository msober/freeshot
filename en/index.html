Features

From shortcut to pinned on screen in three steps.

1Capture

Hover to snap to a window, or drag to select any area. It works across multiple displays.

  • A pixel magnifier for edges accurate to 1 pixel
  • Press C to copy the color under the pointer
  • Return copies to the clipboard, ⌘S saves a file
⌥⌘Astarts a capture

2Annotate

Draw arrows, text and numbered steps right on the screenshot. One swipe of mosaic hides emails and phone numbers.

  • RectangleR
  • EllipseE
  • ArrowA
  • LineL
  • PenP
  • HighlighterH
  • TextT
  • MosaicM
  • NumberN

Made a mistake? ⌘Z undoes it, ⇧⌘Z redoes it.

3Pin

Press ⌘T after a capture and it floats above every window. Write code or fill in a form against it without switching windows.

  • Scroll to zoom, ⌥ and scroll to change opacity
  • ⌥⌘P pins the image or text on your clipboard
  • Press Space to annotate a pin
  • Turn on click-through to use the windows underneath
  • Pin history keeps your last 50 pins to pin again

Privacy

Your screenshots stay on your Mac.

A capture is processed in memory on your Mac and discarded when you finish, unless you copy, save or pin it.

Read the full privacy policy

  • No network

    The app makes no network connections and uploads nothing.

  • No analytics

    No analytics, ads or crash reporting, and no third-party services.

  • No account

    There is nothing to sign up for or log in to. Install it and go.

  • Sandboxed

    Screenshots are saved only where you choose. Pin history stays in the app’s own container.

FAQ

Things you might run into after installing.

If the answer isn’t here, tell us.

Open an issue on GitHub
macOS says FreeShot can’t be opened
The direct download is not notarized by Apple, so the first launch has to be allowed by hand: open FreeShot once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”. On macOS 14, Control-click the app and choose Open.
The screenshot is black, or FreeShot asks for permission
Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording (“Screen & System Audio Recording”), turn on FreeShot, then quit and reopen the app.
I installed it, but where is the app?
FreeShot lives in the menu bar; its icon is a viewfinder. Opening it again from Launchpad or Finder shows its Settings.
A shortcut doesn’t work
Another app may be using the same shortcut. Pick a different one in Settings › Shortcuts.
A pin ignores the mouse
Click-through is on. Click the menu bar icon and choose “Turn Off Click-Through for All Pins”.
