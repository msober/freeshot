FAQ · 常见问题

The screenshot is black or FreeShot asks for permission. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording (“Screen & System Audio Recording”), turn on FreeShot, then quit and reopen the app.
截图黑屏或提示需要权限：打开“系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 屏幕录制（录屏与系统录音）”，开启 FreeShot，然后退出并重新打开应用。

Where is the app? FreeShot lives in the menu bar (the viewfinder icon). Opening it again from Launchpad or Finder shows its Settings.
找不到应用？FreeShot 常驻在菜单栏（取景框图标）。从启动台或访达再次打开会显示设置窗口。

A shortcut doesn’t work. Another app may use the same shortcut. Pick a different one in Settings › Shortcuts.
快捷键无效：可能与其他应用冲突，请在“设置 › 快捷键”中更换。

A pin ignores the mouse. You turned on click-through. Use the menu bar icon › “Turn Off Click-Through for All Pins”.
贴图无法点击：开启了鼠标穿透，可在菜单栏图标中选择“取消所有贴图的鼠标穿透”。
