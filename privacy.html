Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

FreeShot (“the app”) is a screenshot, annotation and pinning tool for macOS. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: FreeShot does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. FreeShot has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party services. The app does not make network connections.

Screen content

With your permission (macOS “Screen Recording”), FreeShot captures the screen only when you start a screenshot. The captured image is processed entirely in memory on your Mac and is discarded when you finish, unless you choose to copy it to the clipboard, save it to a file, or pin it on screen. Nothing is recorded continuously and nothing is uploaded.

Clipboard

FreeShot writes to the clipboard when you copy a screenshot or a color value, and reads the clipboard only when you choose “Pin Clipboard”.

Files

Screenshots are saved only to locations you choose (or the folder you configure in Settings). FreeShot stores its preferences (such as shortcuts and the save folder) locally on your Mac using standard system settings storage.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Please open an issue at github.com/msober/freeshot/issues.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日

FreeShot（“本应用”）是一款 macOS 截图、标注与贴图工具。简而言之：FreeShot 不收集、不存储、不传输、不分享任何个人数据。

我们收集的数据

无。本应用没有账号系统，不包含任何统计、广告、崩溃上报或第三方服务，也不会进行任何网络连接。

屏幕内容

在你授予 macOS“屏幕录制”权限后，FreeShot 仅在你主动截图时截取屏幕。截取的图像只在你的 Mac 内存中处理；除非你选择复制到剪贴板、保存为文件或贴到屏幕上，否则在截图结束后即被丢弃。本应用不会持续录制，也不会上传任何内容。

剪贴板

仅当你复制截图或颜色值时写入剪贴板；仅当你使用“贴图（剪贴板）”时读取剪贴板。

文件

截图只会保存到你选择的位置（或你在设置中指定的文件夹）。快捷键、保存位置等偏好设置通过系统标准方式保存在你的 Mac 本地。

儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，将在此页面公布并注明新的生效日期。
